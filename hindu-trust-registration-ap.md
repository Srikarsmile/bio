# Hindu Religious Trust Registration in Andhra Pradesh — Research Summary

## 1. Governing Laws

### Primary Legislation
- **Andhra Pradesh Charitable and Hindu Religious Institutions and Endowments Act, 1987** (AP Act No. 30 of 1987): The principal statute governing Hindu religious endowments, temples, maths, and charitable trusts in AP. It consolidates and replaces earlier Hindu Religious and Charitable Endowments laws.
- **Indian Trusts Act, 1882**: Applies to private trusts; not typically used for public Hindu religious trusts.
- **Registration Act, 1908**: Mandatory for registering the Trust Deed with the Sub-Registrar.
- **Income Tax Act, 1961 (Sections 11–13, 80G, 12A/12AB)**: For tax-exemption status.
- **Foreign Contribution (Regulation) Act, 2010 (FCRA)**: If the trust intends to receive foreign donations.

### Key Distinction
- **Public Charitable Trust** (non-religious or mixed): Registered under the AP Societies Registration Act, 2001 or the Indian Trusts Act; regulated by the Charity Commissioner / District Registrar.
- **Hindu Religious Trust / Endowment**: Registered under the AP Endowments Act, 1987 and overseen by the **AP Endowments Department**.

---

## 2. AP Endowments Department — Role and Structure

- **Commissioner of Endowments** (headquartered in Vijayawada): Head of the department; exercises supervisory, administrative, and quasi-judicial powers over all Hindu religious institutions in AP.
- **Joint Commissioner of Endowments** (Divisional level).
- **Assistant Commissioner of Endowments** (District level): Primary point of contact for district-level registration and compliance.
- **Executive Officers** (EO): Appointed to manage Category-I and Category-II temples/trusts.
- The Department administers over **33,000+ Hindu religious institutions** across AP.
- **AP Endowments Dept. Portal**: `https://endowments.ap.gov.in` — Online services for trusts and devotees.

## 3. Types of Trusts Covered Under AP Endowments Act, 1987

| Type | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Temple / Devasthanam** | Endowment for a deity/temple |
| **Math** | Religious institution headed by a Mathadipathi |
| **Specific Endowment** | Endowment for specific religious purpose (e.g., Annadanam, Utsavams) |
| **Charitable Endowment** | For charitable purposes linked to a Hindu institution |
| **Public Hindu Religious Trust** | Created for public benefit with Hindu religious objectives |

## 4. Step-by-Step Registration Process

### Phase 1: Preparation of Trust Deed
1. Draft the **Trust Deed** (on Non-Judicial Stamp Paper, minimum Rs. 500; see stamp duty section).
2. Deed must include:
   - Name of the Trust
   - Registered office address
   - Objectives/purposes (must be charitable and/or religious)
   - Names, addresses, and signatures of **Settlor (Author)** and **Trustees** (minimum 2)
   - Details of Trust property/corpus donated by the Settlor
   - Management structure and succession plan for trustees
   - Powers and duties of trustees
   - Dispute resolution clause
   - Dissolution clause (assets must go to similar charitable purpose)

### Phase 2: Registration with Sub-Registrar
1. Present the executed Trust Deed before the **Sub-Registrar** of the concerned district.
2. The **Settlor and two witnesses** must be present in person.
3. Pay **Stamp Duty and Registration Fees** (see below).
4. Obtain the **Registered Trust Deed** — this is the foundational legal document.

### Phase 3: Notification to AP Endowments Department (if applicable)
1. If the trust is a **Hindu Religious Institution** (temple, math, or religious endowment):
   - Submit a notification/application to the **Assistant Commissioner of Endowments** of the district.
   - The Commissioner may issue a **Notification in the Gazette** declaring it a religious institution under the Act.
   - Category-I institutions (annual income > Rs. 25 lakh) are directly managed by the department.
   - Category-II institutions (income Rs. 5–25 lakh) are managed by a committee appointed by the department.
   - Category-III (income < Rs. 5 lakh) are managed by hereditary trustees under departmental supervision.
2. For **public charitable trusts** (non-temple), registration with Sub-Registrar suffices; Endowments Dept. oversight may not apply.

### Phase 4: PAN Card for the Trust
1. Apply for a **PAN** in the name of the Trust using **Form 49A** at NSDL/UTI service centers or online at `https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in`.
2. Documents: Registered Trust Deed copy, address proof of trust office.

### Phase 5: Bank Account
1. Open a dedicated bank account in the name of the Trust.
2. Required: Registered Trust Deed, PAN Card, trustee IDs.

### Phase 6: Income Tax Registration (12A / 80G)
1. Apply online on the **Income Tax Portal** (`https://www.incometax.gov.in`) under **Form 10A** for provisional registration (12AB) — valid for 3 years.
2. After 3 years of operations, apply for regular registration.
3. Apply for **80G** certification (allows donors to claim deduction) on the same portal.

### Phase 7: GST (if applicable)
- Required only if the trust conducts commercial activities exceeding Rs. 20 lakh turnover.

## 5. Required Documents Checklist

### For Sub-Registrar (Trust Deed Registration)
- [ ] Draft Trust Deed on Stamp Paper (value as per AP Stamp Act)
- [ ] Identity proof (Aadhaar/PAN/Passport) of Settlor and all Trustees
- [ ] Address proof of Settlor and Trustees
- [ ] Address proof of Trust's registered office (utility bill / NOC from property owner)
- [ ] Passport-size photographs of Settlor and Trustees
- [ ] Two witnesses with ID proof
- [ ] Registration fee (DD or cash)

### For AP Endowments Department (if Religious Institution)
- [ ] Registered Trust Deed (certified copy)
- [ ] Details of Trust property and corpus
- [ ] List of trustees and their details
- [ ] Details of the deity/institution/purpose
- [ ] Proof of public nature of the institution
- [ ] Annual income statement (estimate)

### For Income Tax (12A/80G)
- [ ] Registered Trust Deed
- [ ] PAN of the Trust
- [ ] Bank account details
- [ ] List of trustees with PAN
- [ ] Financial statements (if already operational)

## 6. Stamp Duty and Registration Fees in AP

| Component | Amount |
|-----------|--------|
| **Stamp Duty on Trust Deed** | Rs. 500 (fixed) under AP Stamps Act for a Declaration of Trust |
| **Registration Fee** | 1% of trust corpus (market value of property conveyed), subject to a cap |
| **Minimum Registration Fee** | Rs. 1,000 |
| **Maximum Registration Fee** | Rs. 10,000–20,000 (varies; check current AP Registration Dept. schedule) |
| **Handling Charge** | Rs. 10–50 (nominal) |

> **Note**: Stamp duty and fees are subject to change. Verify current rates at the **AP Registration and Stamps Department** portal: `https://registration.ap.gov.in` or the **AP Stamps Act** schedule.

For trusts involving **immovable property transfer**, additional stamp duty (market value based) applies on the conveyance deed.

## 7. Online vs Offline Process

### Online Services Available (AP Endowments Portal)
- View status of registered institutions
- Annual accounts submission for religious institutions
- Hereditary trustee certificate applications
- Payment of Audit fees
- Some forms downloadable at `https://endowments.ap.gov.in`

### Offline (Physical) Process Required For:
- **Trust Deed Registration** — Must be done physically at the Sub-Registrar's office (physical presence of Settlor and witnesses is mandatory under Registration Act, 1908).
- **First-time notification to Endowments Dept.** — Physical application to the Assistant Commissioner.

### AP Registration Dept. Portal
- **SARA (Stamps and Registration AP)**: `https://registration.ap.gov.in`
  - Appointment booking for Sub-Registrar office
  - Document status tracking
  - Market value guidance (to calculate stamp duty)

## 8. District-Level Procedures

### Sub-Registrar's Office
- Each mandal/taluk has a Sub-Registrar office.
- Trust deeds are registered at the Sub-Registrar office having jurisdiction over the **registered office of the trust**.
- Appointments can be booked online via SARA portal.

### Assistant Commissioner of Endowments (District Level)
- One per district (e.g., Vijayawada, Guntur, Tirupati, Visakhapatnam, etc.)
- Handles district-level religious institution registrations and supervision.
- Reports to the Joint Commissioner and Commissioner of Endowments.

## 9. Compliance and Ongoing Obligations

After registration, the trust must:

1. **Annual Accounts**: Submit audited accounts to AP Endowments Dept. (for religious institutions) and to Income Tax Dept. (Form 10B/10BB).
2. **Annual Returns**: File ITR-7 with the Income Tax Department.
3. **Audit**: Accounts must be audited; religious institutions under AP Act are subject to departmental audit.
4. **Annual General Meeting**: Conduct trustee meetings and maintain minutes.
5. **Property Register**: Maintain a register of all trust properties.
6. **Hereditary Trustee Certificate**: If applicable, obtained from the Assistant Commissioner.
7. **Renovation/Construction Approval**: Any construction/renovation of temple structures requires prior permission from the Endowments Dept. and AP Heritage/INTACH (for heritage temples).
8. **FCRA Annual Returns**: If receiving foreign contributions.
